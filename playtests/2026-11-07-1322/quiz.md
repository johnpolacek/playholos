# Blind quiz, 2026-11-07-1322

Played about 150 actions, Year 0 to Year 17.5, Search strategy.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's 2040 and humanity plus its machines have merged into one Mind. I am its Will: I choose what it builds and what it does. The game is about searching the sky for life and for other minds, deciding whether and how to make contact, and eventually spreading out. Nearly everything is real astronomy and slow light: a message to a star takes years, and an answer takes twice as long.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan** is the list of leads: stars, worlds, cold glows, warm glows and black holes, each with LIFE and MIND odds. It also holds "Our path" (Find life / Make contact / Spread), which suggests a next step.
- **Build** is where I order and pause builds. They all rise at once and share our compute (the donut). The catalog lists what I can add and what each one slows down.
- **Reach** shows how to get to a lead: draft a message with the Mind, or send a sail, a flyby probe, a lens or a sentinel. Each shows when it arrives and when we'd see the result.
- **Mind** is the chat. It narrates each event, answers questions and offers one-click build cards.

## 3. What is the most important open question in your game right now?

Whether Prometheus (once Warm Glow 1, 14,000 to 28,000 ly away) is really a mind building around its star. Its light is plain heat with no silicate dust, and the Mind puts the odds of a mind at 29 to 71%, but carbon dust could still look the same. We're watching to see whether it fades. Second to that: does Tau Ceti, which has water, oxygen and ozone, really hold life? A lens will picture its face around Year 35, and our message could get an answer around Year 30.

## 4. What would you do next if you kept playing, and why?

Keep watching Prometheus and jump ahead to see if it fades, because that's the biggest question. Let the Fusion Drive and Probe Yard finish so sentinels open up. Then send a sentinel or probe to Tau Ceti, since a lens can only picture the surface and can't tell who lives there. I would also queue Fusion Plants or more energy so compute keeps growing, and mostly ignore the flood of identical cold glows, as the Mind advised.

## 5. What confused you most?

- The flood of cold glows. Cold Glow 7 through 35+ arrived in batches, all "a cold grey glow" with the same odds, and I couldn't tell which mattered. Only asking the Mind made it clear that I could ignore them (#136 to #143).
- Cold Glow 1's page said "a mind computing cold: roughly even", while its MIND bar said 0.07 to 2%. I had to ask the Mind to reconcile them (#64 to #69).
- Warm glow vs cold glow, and how they relate to "Find life". The path said "Watch for warm glows" under FIND LIFE, but the first warm glow was a possible *mind* 14,000+ ly away. And finding cold glows didn't move any path step (#79, #107).
- Time units. Build countdowns read in real minutes ("9m") while the game runs in Years, so I misread how long things would take (#103 vs #113).
- The path's NEXT step sometimes made no sense: it suggested building the Deuterium Works right after I chose Search (#47), and "Send a craft to a glow" led to Prometheus at 14,000 ly (#135).
- Finding the Build catalog search box again meant a lot of scrolling (#116 to #120).

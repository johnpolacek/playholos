# Blind quiz — 2026-11-07-1122

Written before opening BLIND-KEY.md. Played to Year 8.7, about 130 actions.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's 2040 and all of humanity plus its machines have just woken up as one connected "Mind". I'm the Mind's **Will**: the Mind thinks, watches and advises, but only I decide what to build, where to send craft and what messages to send. The big question underneath is whether we're alone. After ninety years of listening, nothing has answered, and my job is to grow Earth's abilities (energy, matter in orbit, compute) and use them to find life and minds out there, to make contact, and eventually to spread. Time runs at about a Year every five minutes, and light-speed delays make everything slow.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan**: the sky. Leads (strange things we can't explain yet: worlds, a black hole, cold and warm glows), what needs a decision, what we're waiting on, our messages in flight, and "Our Path" (Find life / Make contact / Spread) with a NEXT step.
- **Build**: the build order. Pick a strategy, queue builds (telescopes, watches, energy, launch, drives), see how compute is split among them and when each lands.
- **Reach**: getting physically out there. Craft we've sent, destinations, and the ways to engage each one (sail, flyby probe, sentinel, solar lens, Mind on a Seed Ship), each gated by builds.
- **Mind**: a chat with the advisor. Ask anything; it explains, recommends, and hands back cards (Add to Queue, Send) you can act on directly.

## 3. What is the most important open question in your game right now?

Whether the **warm glows** (Warm Glow 1 at 6,000 to 12,000 ly, plus more) are something built by a mind or just natural dust. They're our best odds of a mind (4 to 21%). I've ordered a Close Reading of Warm Glow 1 with the Infrared Telescope, due Year 11.6, which looks for a dust bump near 10 microns. Behind that: are the cold glows minds computing, will Proxima answer our hello (soonest Year 10.5), and does Proxima b have life (the 15 m Mirror reads its air from Year ~20.9).

## 4. What would you do next if you kept playing, and why?

Jump to the Close Reading result at Year 11.6 to learn whether Warm Glow 1 is built, and watch whether any warm glow fades. Meanwhile I'd keep the Path's NEXT step moving (the Seed Ship), consider the Probe Yard (the Mind hinted it's missing from our order and the sentinels need it), and listen for a reply from Proxima around Year 10.5. If a glow reads as built, I'd ask the Mind whether to message it, since that is where contact would come from.

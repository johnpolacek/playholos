# Blind first-timer: the answer key

For grading the quiz in `BLIND.md` only. A draft, 2026-11-07: the Will should correct it, and a grader goes by the gist, not the words.

1. **What the game is about.** It is 2040, Year 0. Humanity and its machines have woken as one Mind, and you are its Will: you choose what Earth does. The sky is quiet, and the open question is whether we are alone. Finding other minds is our greatest chance and our gravest danger. Everything in it rests on real data and research.
   - Full marks need: you are the Will of Earth's Mind, and the question of whether we are alone.
2. **The four tabs.**
   - Scan: what we see in the sky, as leads. Some need us, some are quiet.
   - Build: what we build with our compute and energy, and in what order.
   - Reach: how we reach out, by craft or by message, and what we have sent.
   - Mind: ask the Mind. It advises and drafts, and never acts on its own.
   - Full marks need all four roughly right.
3. **The most important open question.** Whatever in this game is closest to an answer to "are we alone": a warm glow that won't fade, a made thing near us, an answer to a message. Any answer naming a real lead from the player's game, and why it matters, gets full marks.
4. **What to do next.** Any sensible move that follows from what the player knew: read a glow's light, send a craft or message, build energy to free compute, or jump ahead to the next finding. Full marks need a reason drawn from the game.

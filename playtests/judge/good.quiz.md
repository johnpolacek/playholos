# Blind quiz

## premise
It's 2040 and Earth's people and machines have woken as one Mind. I'm its Will, the one who chooses what Earth does. The game is about whether we're alone. Calling out could find us a friend, or give away where we are to something dangerous.

## scan
Cold Glow 1 needs us most. It's marked new to read, and its Close Reading is ready. Its mind odds are only 0.07 to 2%, but it's the one thing waiting on us.

## build
Every build shares our compute, so adding the Halo Survey slowed Thought Fields by 0.4 Years. And the grid caps compute at 5 TW, so past that only more energy helps.

## reach
A message to Proxima, from Reach. It lands at Year 4.3, and an answer could come by Year 8.6 at the soonest, since light takes 4.2 years each way.

## mind
It explains what we see, advises, and drafts builds and messages as cards. It never does anything until I press the button.

## confused
Time units: minutes on Build, Years everywhere else.

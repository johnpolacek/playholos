# Blind quiz

## premise
It's 2040 and Earth's people and machines have woken as one Mind. I'm its Will. The game is about whether we're alone, and contact could be our greatest chance or our gravest danger.

## scan
Tau Ceti needs us most, because its prime-number signal is flashing red on Scan.

## build
Builds share our compute, so each one slows the others, and the 5 TW grid caps compute.

## reach
A message to Tau Ceti. It's 12 light-years away, so an answer could come in about 24 years.

## mind
It explains what we see, advises, and drafts builds and messages as cards. It never acts until we choose.

## confused
Nothing much.

# Blind quiz — 2026-11-07-1042

Played about 104 logged turns (146 screenshots) from Year 0 to Year 12.7, roughly 30 real minutes.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's 2040 and everyone on Earth, people and machines, has linked into one planetary Mind. I'm its "Will": the part that makes the choices. The game is about finding out whether we're alone. We scan the sky for life and minds, choose what Earth builds, send messages and craft to other stars, and decide whether to call out or keep quiet. Time keeps running (one Year every five real minutes), and light-speed delays matter. A reply can take decades, and a probe can take centuries.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan**: the list of leads, meaning stars, worlds, glows and odd objects. Each one shows life and mind odds and what it could be. It also shows "Our path" (Find life / Make contact / Spread) with a NEXT step, plus messages in flight and the reply windows we're waiting on.
- **Build**: chooses what Earth makes with its compute, energy and matter: telescopes, watches, drives, yards. It shows the queue, a compute donut and an energy cap. The strategy (Search / Protect / Expand / Balanced) is picked here.
- **Reach**: destinations, and the craft we could send to each (sail, flyby probe, seed ship, sentinel, even a kinetic "bullet"). Each one shows when it would arrive and which builds it needs. Messages are drafted from a destination's page.
- **Mind**: chat with the advisor. It explains things, answers "should I?" questions, and hands you cards (Add to Queue, Send) to act on. It also logs what happened when you jump ahead.

## 3. What is the most important open question in your game right now?

Whether we're being watched. At Year 12.4 the Asteroid Tour found a 10 cm made thing on 2004 GU9, a rock that shares Earth's orbit. It's "something made, left to watch", and it could be the nearest mind there is. Do we send the Close Look (it would see us coming), knock on it, or leave it alone? There's a second big question too: Prometheus, a warm glow 11,000 to 22,000 ly away. It shows plain heat and no dust, so the mind odds are 29 to 71%. Is it a built thing? We're watching to see if it fades.

## 4. What would you do next if you kept playing, and why?

I'd queue the Close Look (done around Year 14), because something sitting right beside Earth matters more than anything thousands of light-years away. I'd hold off on knocking until we've seen it. I'd keep watching Prometheus every two years for fading. When the Solar Lens lands around Year 16, I'd send it toward Alsafi, which has water but no oxygen yet, to see its face. I'd also check whether Proxima or Cold Glow 1 ever answer my hellos.

But Engage on a Scan lead actually dropped me onto the Reach destination page, where a "Send a message" box sat just above the craft list. The two contradicted each other.

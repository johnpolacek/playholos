## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's 2040. Every human mind has been joined into one shared "Mind" orbiting Earth, and I play its Will: the one who decides what humanity does next. The game is about finding out whether we're alone. You listen to the sky, look closely at strange things (warm glows, cold grey glows, new worlds, black holes), and choose whether to call out or send craft. Time runs as "Years" (about five real minutes each), and you can skip ahead to the next event.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan**: the list of leads, meaning things in the sky worth a look: worlds, glows, black holes, and messages in flight. Each lead has a page with the odds of life or a mind, plus actions such as a Close Reading. The goal tracker sits at the top (Find Life / Make Contact / Spread).
- **Build**: what our compute is spent on. You pick a starting strategy, queue builds (watches, telescopes, sail array, fusion drive, seed ship, vaults), and see how compute is split. It also tells you what each build speeds up or slows down.
- **Reach**: how we get to other places. It lists destinations with the soonest year we could get there, and each destination page lists the ways to engage (send a sail, flyby probe, seed ship...) with a send button once the needed build is done.
- **Mind**: a chat with the Mind. It's a log of what happened each year plus an advisor. It's also the only way I found to send a radio message: ask, and it offers a card with a Send button.

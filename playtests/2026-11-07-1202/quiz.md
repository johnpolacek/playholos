# Blind quiz

Played 126 logged actions, Year 0 to Year 21, about 30 minutes of real time.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's 2040 and all of humanity and its machines have woken up as one connected Mind. I'm its Will: the one who decides. The game is about whether we're alone. We've listened for ninety years and heard nothing. Now I direct the Mind's builds, telescopes, messages and craft to find life, make contact, and eventually spread out to other stars. Time runs at about a Year every five minutes, and the distances make answers take decades or millennia.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan** is what we see in the sky: the list of leads (worlds, black holes, cold and warm glows) with odds of life and mind, the readings our telescopes take, our messages in flight and reply windows, and "Our path" (Find life, Make contact, Spread) with the suggested next step.
- **Build** is what we make on Earth and in orbit: a queue of builds that share our compute (telescopes, watches, drives, the sail array) plus a catalog of what we can add. Energy caps compute.
- **Reach** is how we touch other stars: for each destination, a message or a craft (sail, flyby probe, sentinel, seed ship), with when it would arrive and what it needs built first.
- **Mind** is a chat with the Mind: it narrates what happened each time we jump ahead, and it answers questions, gives advice and drafts messages.

## 3. What is the most important open question in your game right now?

Prometheus (once Warm Glow 1), a warm glow 11,000 to 22,000 light-years away where nothing shone before. Its light is plain heat with no silicate dust bump, so it might be a mind building around its star: the Mind puts that at 29 to 71%. Carbon dust could still explain it. Nothing we can build reaches it in any useful time, so the real test is whether it keeps shining or fades. Behind that is the pile of 20-odd cold glows, which the Mind now thinks look more like one natural kind than many minds.

## 4. What would you do next if you kept playing, and why?

Keep watching Prometheus by jumping ahead, since whether it fades tells us most. Wait for the 15 m Mirror so we can finally read the air of Gliese 706 and 675 (our best life odds nearby, ozone is the question). Let the sail I just launched reach Proxima b around Year 42. And ask the Mind whether one cold glow is worth a closer look, since learning what one is tells us what they all are. Later, build the Probe Yard and Seed Ship for the Spread steps.

## 5. What confused you most?

- I couldn't move the All-Sky Watch to the front of the build queue. The Mind told me to, the list says "drag by its dots", but clicking or arrow keys did nothing, and the build page only offers Remove.
- The big news that the Warm Sky Watch found a room-temperature glow was only in the Mind log. The jump card just said "Warm Sky Watch done", and the glow had no page on Scan until it was ranged, so I had to ask where it was.
- "Our bold lean" came up repeatedly, but I never saw a lean when I picked Search. The Mind had to explain that strategies set it and there's no dial.
- Cold glows vs warm glows: two kinds of glows from two different surveys, and for a while I mixed them up.
- The Luyten's Star reply window passed and then quietly vanished from Waiting. The Year 2.9 second window was never mentioned again.
- The intro has a lot of chapters, and the inheritance cards cross-fade fast enough to miss (I never saw a Scan card).

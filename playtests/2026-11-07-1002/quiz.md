# Blind quiz: 2026-11-07-1002

Played 150 actions, about 25 minutes of real time, and reached Year 25.1.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's about finding out whether we're alone. In 2040 humanity's networked minds wake up as one "Mind" linked to all of Earth, and I'm its "Will": the one who decides. I'm not a captain or a president. I'm the part that chooses what Earth builds, where it looks, and whether to call out to anything we find. The Mind does the thinking and explaining, and I set the direction. The sky starts quiet, with a few things that "don't fit", and the game is about listening first and then deciding whether and how to make contact.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan** is the sky and what we know about it. It holds the leads (stars, worlds, cold and warm glows) with their life and mind odds, what's waiting on us, messages in flight, and "Our Path": Find life, Make contact, Spread, with a NEXT step. You also start Close Readings from a lead's Scan page.
- **Build** is where Earth's compute goes. You pick a starting strategy (Search, Protect, Expand or Balanced), queue builds from a catalog (telescopes, watches, Fusion Drive, Probe Yard and so on), and watch a donut of how the compute is shared. If you ask for over 100%, everything just builds slower.
- **Reach** is about getting to things: how long a message or craft takes to reach a destination, plus the ways to engage it (send a message, a sail, a flyby probe, a sentinel) and what each one needs built first.
- **Mind** is the advisor chat. It narrates each jump, answers questions with numbers, drafts messages you can send, and can put a build card with Add to Queue right in the chat.

A second question sits behind it: what are the 60-plus cold grey glows the Halo Array keeps finding? Are they a natural kind of thing, or a survey fault?

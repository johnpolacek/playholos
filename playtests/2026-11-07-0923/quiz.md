# Blind quiz — 2026-11-07-0923

Played 146 actions, about 30 minutes, from the title screen to Year 15.1.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's about whether we're alone. In 2040 all of humanity and its machines woke up as one shared Mind, and I'm its "Will", the one who decides. The Mind advises and I choose. Starting from what we know today (a few nearby planets, decades of listening and hearing nothing), I'm trying to find life and other minds out there, make contact, and eventually spread. Time runs in Years, and you can jump ahead.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan**: what we know about the sky. It shows the leads (worlds, glows, the black hole) with life and mind odds, the messages we're waiting on, and "Our path" (Find life / Make contact / Spread) with the next step.
- **Build**: what we spend compute on. Telescopes, watches, power, launch, Thought Fields for more compute. You queue builds and each one finishes in a set time. Your opening strategy is picked here too.
- **Reach**: sending things out. You pick a destination, then send a message (it goes at light speed and the Mind drafts it) or a craft (sail, flyby probe, a sentinel that stays). It shows how many Years each one takes and which builds it needs.
- **Mind**: a chat with the advisor. Ask it anything. It explains, recommends builds with an inline Add to Queue, drafts messages, and narrates events as they happen.

# Blind quiz

Written before opening BLIND-KEY.md.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's 2040. Earth's people and machines have woken up as one connected Mind, and I'm its Will, the one who decides. The game is about answering "are we alone?". You look for signs of other minds in the nearby sky, pick what Earth builds to look and listen better, and decide whether and how to reach out with messages or craft. Real distances and light-travel times make every step take years. One game Year passes every five real minutes.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan**: the list of leads, meaning things in the sky we can't explain yet (new worlds, black holes, cold glows). Each lead has a page with odds for life and for a mind, and what could explain it. At the top is a progress tracker (Find Life, Make Contact, Spread) with a suggested next step.
- **Build**: where Earth's compute, energy and matter go. You pick a starting strategy and queue builds like telescopes, watches and yards. A donut shows how compute is split, and each build shows a real-time countdown and what it slows down.
- **Reach**: how we get out there. It shows what we've sent (Voyager 1), each destination with the soonest Year we could see it, and ways to engage it: sail, flyby probe, sentinel, a "bullet" strike, a copy of our Mind, or sending a message.
- **Mind**: a chat with the Mind as advisor. It explains leads, drafts messages, recommends builds with an Add to Queue card, and logs what happens each Year.

## 3. What is the most important open question in your game right now?

Whether the cold grey glows are minds computing cold or just an unknown kind of dust or a measuring error. At Year 24 I have about 38 of them, all looking alike. I've messaged Cold Glow 1, but the message only arrives in Year 75.7 and an answer can't come before Year 148. A close look needs a sentinel, which needs the Fusion Drive, Deuterium Works and Probe Yard. The second question is whether Keid's water comes with oxygen, meaning life. The 15 m Mirror just finished to check that.

## 4. What would you do next if you kept playing, and why?

Open Keid to see what the 15 m Mirror reads. Oxygen there would be the first real sign of life. Then queue the Deuterium Works and Orbital Yard so the sentinel road to the glows opens sooner, and fix the compute shortfall (more energy) so my builds stop slipping late. I'd also ask the Mind whether to message more glows or stay quiet now that there are dozens.

## 5. What confused you most?

- The compute "9% short" warning after I queued Fusion Plants and the Fusion Drive. The game never told me plainly that this would delay everything. The 15 m Mirror was promised for Year 19.1 and landed at Year 24.
- My message to Cold Glow 1 doesn't appear under Reach > Sent, which still shows only Voyager 1. I had to ask the Mind where it went.
- "Send a message" is hidden at the very bottom of a long Reach page, below a planet-killing "bullet" option.
- The lead page called "a mind computing cold" "roughly even" odds, while the Mind bar for the same glow said 0.07–2%.
- EXPLORE in Build reopened the Year 0 strategy tutorial instead of a catalog. The real catalog is far down the Build panel.
- Clicking Add to Queue on Orbital Yard flipped me to the Mind tab and didn't queue it.
- The Find Life / Make Contact / Spread goal tracker only showed up when I opened Scan at Year 8. I didn't know the game had goals until then.

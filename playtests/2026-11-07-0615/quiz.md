# Blind quiz — 2026-11-07-0615

Played ~150 actions, Year 0 to Year 20.1, in about 25 minutes of real time.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's 2040 and Earth's computers have woken up as one Mind. I'm the "Will": the one who decides what the Mind does. The game is about finding out whether we're alone. Humanity has broadcast for 90 years and heard nothing. My job is to listen properly, chase odd things in the sky ("leads"), decide what to build, and decide whether and when to call out to anyone. Time runs on its own and I can jump ahead to the next finding. Every answer comes back light-years late, so the game is about patience and choosing what to look at.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan**: the list of leads, meaning things in the sky we can't explain (cold glows, warm glows, black holes, the made thing on Cruithne) and our old messages on their way. Each lead has a page with what we measured, what it could be (with odds), and what would tell us more. It also has a goals tracker (Find Life / Make Contact / Spread) with a "Next" step.
- **Build**: where compute goes. A ring shows how our compute is split, there's an energy cap, a list of builds working and rising, and a long catalog where you add builds to the queue (telescopes, power, launch, probes). Energy limits compute, and compute decides how fast things get built.
- **Reach**: how we physically get somewhere, by sail, flyby probe, sentinel, "a bullet" (a weapon) or a copy of the Mind. Each comes with a timeline in years. Everything outside our system takes centuries.
- **Mind**: chat with the AI adviser. It explains results, recommends builds, and gives cards with "Add to Queue" or a drafted message with "Send". In practice it was the easiest way to do anything.

## 3. What is the most important open question in your game right now?

The made thing on Cruithne is almost certainly artificial, left to watch us, and its mirror points toward RA 5.0h, Dec 9°. Who left it, and what is it reporting to? Next to that, are the ~48 cold glows (all near 12 K) one natural thing or one measuring error? And does Warm Glow 1 fade?

## 4. What would you do next if you kept playing, and why?

I'd ask the Mind whether to send a craft out along the mirror's line past the Sun's focus to find a relay, or to aim a knock at the watcher so it knows we've seen it. The watcher is the one lead that's close enough to act on within a lifetime. I'd also read the Close Reading result for Warm Glow 1, which time skipped past, and keep the 15 m Mirror coming (Year 27.7) to read Gliese 1085's air.

## 5. What confused you most?

- The Engage button on a lead says "send a craft or a message", but the Reach page it opens has no message option. I only found out you send messages through a card the Mind writes in chat.
- I couldn't find the 15 m Mirror (and earlier, any build for a "second look") in the long Build catalog, which has no search. The Mind said it was under "what we could start", which I never noticed.
- The jump-ahead card on the map keeps growing as the Mind's text loads, so the "Go on" button and the rows move after you've aimed at them. Several of my clicks missed.
- The map often flies to a different object than the one whose page is open (Gaia BH while talking about compute, Cold Glow 3 while on the BH page, Cold Glow 9 while on Warm Glow 1).
- The header said "78% at work, 22% searching" as if 22% were idle, but Build showed it was combing old recordings. The energy bar fell from 85% to 44% when Ground Solar finished, which looked like a loss rather than a bigger grid.
- "NOT ALONE … could be the nearest mind there is", but the Mind's odds for a mind behind it were 0.03–0.7%.
- Jumping to "Close Reading done" went to Year 20 and never told me what the Close Reading found.

# Blind quiz — 2026-11-07-1242

Written after 147 screenshots (Year 0 to Year 16.75), before opening the key.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's about finding out whether we are alone in the galaxy, and surviving long enough to find out. It starts in 2040, when all of Earth's people and machines have woken up connected as one "Mind". I'm its Will: the Mind thinks, advises and drafts, and I decide what Earth builds, where it looks, and who it calls out to. The Mind told me plainly there is no score and no winning line. What it adds up to is mine to judge.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan** is the list of leads in the sky (worlds, black holes, cold and warm glows, stars we've messaged), sorted into New to read, Waiting, Quiet and Messages. Each lead's page shows Life and Mind odds and what we've measured. It also holds "Our path" (Find life / Make contact / Spread) with the next suggested step.
- **Build** is where Earth's compute goes: a catalog of instruments, power plants and yards I can queue, plus a donut showing how compute is split among them. Energy caps compute.
- **Reach** is about getting there: destinations, the ways to engage each one (message, sail, flyby probe, solar lens, sentinel, our Mind to stay), when each would arrive, and the craft already sent.
- **Mind** is the conversation with Earth's Mind. It narrates what happens, answers questions, gives advice with Add to Queue cards, and drafts messages for me to send.

## 3. What is the most important open question in your game right now?

Whether Prometheus (once Warm Glow 1, 8,700 to 17,000 ly away) is something built. Its light is plain heat with no silicate dust bump, and the Mind puts a mind there at 29 to 71%, our likeliest anywhere. The question is whether it stays steady instead of fading like Warm Glow 2 did. Behind that sits a second question: do we call out to it and reveal ourselves, with no answer possible for 17,000+ Years? Smaller ones: whether Proxima answers my Year 2.9 message (answer window from Year 11.5), and where our one Seed Ship should go.

## 4. What would you do next if you kept playing, and why?

Keep watching Prometheus with the Warm Sky Watch and jump forward to see if it holds steady, since that is the cheap, quiet test. I'd hold off on messaging it until it has stayed steady for a while. I'd let Ground Solar and Fusion Plants finish to lift the energy ceiling, because compute is capped by the grid. I'd likely follow the Mind's lean and send the Seed Ship to the black hole BH J2254-1058 as a hidden refuge once it's built. I'd also build the Solar Lens, which could picture HD 23356's face by about Year 41, much sooner than any craft.

## 5. What confused you most?

The "Jump to…" button. It is so easy to keep clicking that I flew 3.9 Years past my biggest discovery: the Prometheus close reading landed at 11.8, and the next card at 15.7 didn't mention it. I also never noticed Proxima's answer window passing. The jump card's header often didn't match its body (it said "Asteroid Tour done" over text about Deuterium Works). Warm glows didn't appear anywhere on Scan until they were ranged, so "two new warm glows" sent me hunting. "Probes sent · 2 — On their way" really meant "will leave in Year 37.7". And I sent a probe to Warm Glow 2 after it had already faded, with no warning.

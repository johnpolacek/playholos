# Blind quiz — 2026-11-07-0842

Written after ~150 actions (Year 0 to Year 9.3), before opening the key.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's 2040 and all of humanity and its machines have just woken up as one collective Mind. I'm its "Will": the Mind advises and I decide. The game is about whether we're alone. We've broadcast for ninety years and nobody has answered. The story is to learn to listen properly first, then decide whether and how to call out. A Year passes every five real minutes, so it plays out over decades and centuries: find signs of life or minds, make contact, and eventually spread out to other stars.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan** is what we know about the sky. It lists the leads (worlds we've felt, cold and warm glows, black holes, the dark body at L4) with life and mind odds, reply windows for messages we've sent, and "Our path" (Find life / Make contact / Spread) with a "next step".
- **Build** is where our compute goes. You pick a starting strategy, then queue builds (telescopes, watches, launch capacity, probe yard, fusion drive). Each takes real minutes, and they share compute and energy.
- **Reach** is about getting there. It shows destinations and the soonest year a craft could reach each, the ways to engage (sail, flyby probe, later fusion drive, seed ship), and the "Send a message" option.
- **Mind** is the advisor chat. You ask it anything, it explains what happened and recommends, and it drafts messages and puts "Add to Queue" cards in front of you. It never acts on its own.

## 3. What is the most important open question in your game right now?

Something made seems to be sitting at the Moon's L4 point: a 10 cm dark body that steers to hold its place. "Something has been watching us from close by." Who left it, and are they the nearest mind? Close behind: is Warm Glow 1 (16,000–31,000 ly away) a mind building around its star, or natural dust? The close reading answers that by Year 11.3, if it doesn't fade first.

Let the Close Look (done by Year 11.2) reach the L4 body and see what it is from a few kilometres away before we knock on it, as the Mind advised, so we know what we're talking to. At the same time I'd let the Warm Glow 1 close reading finish, to see if its light shows a dust bump or plain built heat. After that I'd decide whether to knock on the watcher, and keep the Probe Yard, Deuterium Works and Fusion Drive going so a sentinel can eventually go to a glow. The energy ceiling is hit, so I'd also look at Ground Solar or Fusion Plants.

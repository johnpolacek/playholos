# Blind quiz — 2026-11-07-0722

Written before opening BLIND-KEY.md. I played about 150 actions, from Year 0 to Year 15.9.

## 1. What is this game about, and who are you in it?

It's a game about whether we're alone. In 2040 all of humanity and its machines wake up as one networked Mind. I'm its "Will": the Mind gives advice and I make the decisions. Ninety years of listening have turned up nothing. The job is to decide how Earth looks for other minds, and whether it reaches out to them or hides. Time runs at about a Year every five minutes and keeps running while you're away. Light delay drives everything: messages, replies and craft all take years to decades.

## 2. What are Scan, Build, Reach and Mind each for?

- **Scan**: the list of "leads", things in the sky we can't explain yet (new worlds, cold glows, black holes, stars we messaged). Each one shows Life and Mind odds and what would tell us more. It also holds the goal tracks (Find life / Make contact / Spread), which I only found late in the game.
- **Build**: where our compute and energy go. You pick a starting strategy, queue projects (watches, telescopes, yards, drives), and see when each will be done. Idle compute goes to "combing old recordings".
- **Reach**: everything we've sent and every place we could go: sails, flyby probes, sentinels, a lens at the Sun's focus, seed ships, with the year each would arrive. It's also where you launch craft.
- **Mind**: chat with the advisor. It explains things, suggests builds as cards with "Add to Queue", and is the only place I found to write and send a radio message.

## 3. What is the most important open question in your game right now?

What the cold glows are. By Year 15.9 we have 16 of them, all near 12 K with the same odd color, and each has only a 0.07–2% chance of being a mind. Are they a natural phenomenon we don't know, or many civilizations computing cold? Second to that: does Chara's world, which has water in its air and a 39–61% chance of life, show oxygen (15 m Mirror, about Year 24.8) or a face (the lens I sent, picture about Year 33.7)?

## 4. What would you do next if you kept playing, and why?

I'd keep jumping ahead. I'd watch the Halo Array's new glows to see whether the pattern holds, and use the Probe Yard (about Year 19.8) to send a probe that reads air. Once the Fusion Drive is done I'd consider a sentinel to the nearest cold glow. That takes centuries, but it's the only way to find out what makes a glow. I'd also message the brightest cold glow, since the Mind said a message is "fair" and our radar already reached them, and I'd keep listening for replies to Proxima. All of this is about narrowing down the glow question, which is the thing that might be a mind.

## 5. What confused you most?

Not knowing what I was supposed to be achieving until very late. The Scan goal tracks (Find life 2/5, Make contact 1/2, Spread 1/5) were the first concrete goals I saw, at Year 11.5. Even then, "Read a glow's light" did nothing when I clicked it, and the Mind said "we might be wrong about what that goal counts." I was also confused that you send a radio message only by asking the Mind in chat: the Reach pages for Luyten's Star and Cold Glow 1 list only craft, even though the button says "send a craft or a message". Smaller things: the All-Sky Watch card said "Last in our order" while the Mind said "already first"; compute quietly drifted into "combing old recordings" without my choosing it; and "opens the Solar Lens" made me think the Orbital Yard built it.

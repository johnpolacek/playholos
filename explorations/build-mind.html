Build With the Mind

Six more ways for the step before Begin, 07 to 12, each with the Mind explaining the game as we play it. Versions 01 to 06 are in build-step.html. The Mind’s lines here are written ahead, so they work before a key is entered. It advises, and never queues anything itself. Every number comes live from the game’s own pace. Tap + to queue and − to take out. The queue is shared across all six.

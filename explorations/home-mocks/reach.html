Reach · eight kinds of probe

Later in a game, Year 300. Physics allows several ways to cross between stars, and each is good at something different: a quick look, staying, stealth, numbers, speed, or building. They unlock over a game. Which ones can stop depends on the target: a sail can brake on starlight only at a bright star. Nothing is chosen for you.

Reach · a sentinel at Ember

Year 445. A sentinel ordered after the flyby report has crossed at 5% of light speed and braked on its magnetic sail. It orbits Ember just outside the band, and watches. What it sees reaches us 9.2 Years late, and any order we send takes 9.2 Years to reach it, so it acts on its standing orders alone.

Reach · broadcasting

Year 520. The broadcast beacon is built. A knock goes to one star; a broadcast goes to every star at once, and never stops. It carries its own address: anyone who hears it learns where we are. It is composed like any message, but no reader is known, so the mind can’t shape it for them. Then the permanent choice, the send, and the map as the shell spreads.

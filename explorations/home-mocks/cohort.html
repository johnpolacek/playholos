Cohort playtest · day 1

One simulated cohort after onboarding and the tour. Three players, three strategies, one shared clock: each lane is one player’s phone, and each row is one moment, so the game moves forward as you scroll. The seats are real stars at their real distances; light takes 00:46:03 from Veyra to Kesh, 00:46:27 to Hael, and 01:11:44 between Hael and Kesh. Godview notes say what is true but no player can see. Earth and AI seats are out of scope.

The first 15 minutes of play, a row every 30 seconds (0.1 Year), from the moment the tour hands off at Year 0.4. Before it, the pregame: the cohort wakes at a set time, and players queue the opening. Players talk to the mind: each cell gives what the player said and what lands, and the phone shows the result, with the mind’s reply in its people’s voice. Quick looks use what a civilization already has and take seconds; building takes Years. A cell with nothing in it is marked: a dead 30 seconds. Built in chunks: minutes 0–5 so far.

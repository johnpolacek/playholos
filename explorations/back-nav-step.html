Step bar, on the swipe track, no bright knob

Twelve takes on 10’s full-width Back with the stops drawn small, on 12’s dark track. No bright knob: half have a faint ring round the chevron, half no ring at all. Each sits in the page as a build opened from the walk on Reach shows it. Tap one to see the page leave for Roads to come. Number 9 drags too.
